GOT QUESTIONS?

FAQ

Not at all! Tandem skydiving requires zero prior experience. Your certified instructor handles all technical aspects while you simply enjoy the ride. Ground training takes 45 minutes and covers everything you need to know.

Jumpers must be at least 18 years old (ID required). Maximum weight is 220 lbs (100 kg) due to equipment limits. There's no strict upper age limit — we've had jumpers in their 80s! Consult your doctor if you have any medical concerns.

Tandem skydiving has an excellent safety record — roughly 1 fatality per 500,000 jumps. SkyRush maintains a perfect safety record over 50,000+ jumps. All instructors hold USPA Tandem Instructor ratings, and equipment is rigorously inspected before every jump.

Comfortable, form-fitting clothes suitable for the weather. Wear closed-toe sneakers (no sandals or boots). Avoid baggy clothes that could flap in the wind. We provide jumpsuits, goggles, helmets, and all necessary gear.

Yes! We offer free rescheduling up to 48 hours before your jump. Cancellations made 7+ days out receive a full refund. Weather cancellations are always rescheduled at no cost — your safety comes first.

We monitor conditions closely and make go/no-go calls on the day. Jumps are cancelled if wind exceeds limits, cloud cover is too thick, or visibility is poor. You'll be contacted early and offered a free reschedule. Safety over schedules — always.
