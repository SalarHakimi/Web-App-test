Everything You Need to Know

Not at all. Tandem skydiving requires zero experience. Your instructor manages all technical aspects while you simply enjoy the ride. Ground training covers everything in 45 minutes.

Jumpers must be 18+ (ID required). Maximum weight is 220 lbs due to equipment limits. There's no upper age limit — we've had jumpers in their 80s. Consult your doctor if you have any medical concerns.

Tandem skydiving has roughly 1 fatality per 500,000 jumps. SkyRush has a perfect record over 50,000+ jumps. All instructors hold USPA Tandem Instructor ratings, and all rigs are inspected before every jump.

Comfortable, form-fitting clothes appropriate for the weather. Closed-toe sneakers are required — no boots, sandals, or flip-flops. We provide the jumpsuit, helmet, goggles, and all equipment.

Free rescheduling up to 48 hours before your jump. Full refund for cancellations 7+ days out. Weather cancellations are always rescheduled at no cost. Your safety always comes before the schedule.
